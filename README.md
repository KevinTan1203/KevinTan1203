## Hi there 👋

I'm **Kevin Tan** — a **Digital Assets & FX Trader** with a background in software engineering and quantitative analysis.

I work at the intersection of financial markets, quantitative finance, and technology. My current role focuses on institutional OTC crypto and FX execution, while I continue to research data-driven trading strategies, market microstructure, and systematic portfolio construction in my own time.

Previously, I was a Software Engineer in Global Markets & Enterprise Technology at **United Overseas Bank (UOB)**, building production systems that support institutional trading and shared platform infrastructure.

---

### 💼 Current Focus

- Institutional OTC cryptocurrency & FX trading
- Basis-point pricing, VWAP execution & cross-venue arbitrage
- Market-neutral hedging and trade lifecycle management
- Quantitative research: statistical modelling, systematic strategies & risk management
